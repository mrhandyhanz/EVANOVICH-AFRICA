Our Story

Faith In Action. Hope In Communities.

I Know Evanovich Africa Foundation exists to restore dignity, opportunity and sustainable living to vulnerable children and families across Uganda.

Our Mission

To provide clean water, quality education, healthcare and nutritional support through faith-driven community action.

Our Vision

A future where every child has the opportunity to thrive, learn and live with dignity.

Our Faith Foundation

Our work is inspired by Christian values of compassion, stewardship and service. We believe every person carries God-given dignity and deserves access to opportunity, hope and care.
